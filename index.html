Recommended Folder Structure

Folder names are a suggestion, but be consistent with your naming conventions' information design. The parent folder (mediatedExpression-DMS2610) shouldn't be a repository, but a folder local to your computer. Folders the next level down (templates-DMS2610, studioWorkIndex-DMS2610 etc) should all be repositories.
