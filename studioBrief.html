Studio Project Brief : Mediated Expression

RMIT Digital Media Studio 2610

Design and build a small interactive expressive tool for web browsers, designed to fit a specified creative context of your choice.

Your creative tool should be of limited scope and experiment with the novel application of at least one of the following interaction design principles:

  • Information design
  • Feedback
  • Feedback

Your creative tool must be designed and built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and optionally extended with use of the Konva.js and/or Tone.js external JavaScript libraries.

Any use of additional/alternative JavaScript libraries must be negotiated before your Peer User Testing.

You may also include additional media from external sources, such as images, audio and video, only if they are under an appropriate copyright licence and are properly attributed in your work.
