Mediated Expression : Generominos Exercise

RMIT Digital Media Studio 2610

Generominos

This is a construction set for creating generative interactive art and games, created by Kate Compton.

Brief:

Work with your group to respond to the context of your provided scenario card. At the end of the exercise your group will present your system to the rest of the class.

Instructions:

  1. Follow the instructions on the first page of the set to construct a system that responds to the given context. At the end please name your system and your team. Below are some tips on managing the exercise within the given timeline.
  2. Remember this is a team exercise : have a quick discussion around assigning roles, and or expectations of contribution.
  3. Work to roughly sketch out the design context and potential response by thinking about judgements of purpose and worth.
  4. Framing will also be especially important! There's a lot of cards, and you will only use a relatively small number of them, so think about who will look through what, how to organise the cards, and how to rule out ones you won't need.
  5. A good place to start is with the output, then think about possible inputs, before working out what process of interpolation you need to reach one from the other.
  6. Don't just pick the first path, think about experimenting with novel options, and how these each change the work.
  7. When you think that you're done, draw the connections on the paper, and then think about your project and team names.
